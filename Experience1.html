My ExperienceBuilding A Website

Professional journey and projects I have worked on.

Back to Home

I'm Chelsea Valencia Anugrah Widya. Last March, I decided to create my very first website. I had always been interested in technology and design, but I felt unsure and afraid that I would not be able to do it well. However, I finally gathered my courage and started preparing everything I needed. I collected many references from various interesting websites, learned the basics of HTML and CSS through online tutorials, and wrote down important notes to guide me along the way.

When the time came to build it, I sat in front of my laptop full of enthusiasm, but things did not go smoothly at first. The codes I wrote often had errors, and the display looked messy and completely different from what I had imagined. I felt frustrated and even wanted to give up several times, but I kept reminding myself of my goal. I carefully checked every line of code, fixed the mistakes one by one, and sometimes asked for help from friends who understood programming better than I did.

After working hard for three days, my website finally worked perfectly just as I planned. It contains my personal portfolio and the stories I have written myself. I felt so happy and proud of what I had achieved, and my family and friends also praised my work. This experience taught me that nothing is impossible if we keep trying and never give up easily. Now, I am motivated to learn more and create even better websites in the future.
